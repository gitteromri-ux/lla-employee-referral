FAQ

Questions worth answering.

01Who can participate in the referral program?
Every eTeacher Group employee, across all of our schools and offices, in every country. Your referred student can live anywhere too — The Longevity Blueprint is taught live online.
02How and when do I get my $300?
Your reward is confirmed once the person you referred completes enrollment in The Longevity Blueprint. Payouts are processed by HR/payroll in the pay cycle after enrollment is confirmed. There is no cap — every enrolled referral earns you $300.
03Who is the course for?
For ambitious people who want to optimize their health, energy, and longevity through science-backed nutrition, exercise, recovery, and biomarker tracking. Perfect for high performers, wellness enthusiasts, and anyone ready to take a proactive approach to living longer and feeling better.
04Does my friend need a medical background?
No. The course is designed for anyone interested in improving their health, energy, and longevity. No medical, scientific, or fitness background is needed — complex topics are broken down into practical, easy-to-understand concepts applied through simple tracking, actionable exercises, and personalized insights.
05What happens in a typical session?
Each week is a 50-minute live session with expert instructors and a community of like-minded people. Sessions combine science-backed learning, real biomarker and CGM data analysis, and interactive discussion around nutrition, movement, sleep, and recovery — and every student leaves with a personalized experiment to run during the week.
06How much does the course cost?
Two plans, same program: $289/month across 5 monthly installments ($1,445 total), or a one-time upfront payment of $1,249 (saving $196). Tuition includes all 18 live sessions, full platform access, the continuous glucose monitor, and shipping. Seats are limited, first-come, first-served.
07What equipment does my friend need?
Just a laptop or tablet for live sessions and a smartphone to track progress. The continuous glucose monitor is included with enrollment and ships directly to their door, arriving before Lesson 5, when the CGM portion of the program begins. Smartwatches and other wearables are completely optional.
08Can they catch up if they miss a session?
Absolutely. All sessions are recorded and available on demand. Attending live is recommended for the expert guidance and class discussion, but every session and all course materials can be reviewed whenever their schedule allows.
09Is this medical advice?
No. The program is educational and focused on understanding the science of longevity, nutrition, movement, recovery, and biomarker tracking. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical care. For medical concerns or clinical decisions, students should continue working with their physician.
